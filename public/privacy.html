Privacy Policy

VOOXOR respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the VOOXOR website and how that information is handled.

When you submit a video URL, VOOXOR processes that URL to provide the requested download service. We do not require you to create an account to use the service.

The exact retention period may depend on the hosting provider and infrastructure configuration.

VOOXOR is a tool for processing publicly accessible media URLs supplied by users. VOOXOR does not claim ownership of downloaded content. You are responsible for ensuring that you have the necessary rights or permission to download and use any content submitted to the service.

Third-Party Services

VOOXOR may rely on third-party hosting, infrastructure, or media processing services to operate the website. These providers may process technical information as necessary to provide their services and are subject to their own policies and terms.

Data Retention

VOOXOR is designed to minimize unnecessary storage of submitted media. Temporary processing files may be created while a download is being prepared and are intended to be removed after processing. Technical logs may be retained according to the policies and configuration of the hosting infrastructure.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights concerning personal information, including rights to access, correction, deletion, or information about how your data is processed. Requests can be directed to the operator of the VOOXOR service.

Children's Privacy

VOOXOR is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. Users should not submit personal information through the service.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the service, its features, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will always be published on this page.

Contact
