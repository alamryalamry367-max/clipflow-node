FAQ

Built for compliant use.

Can I paste any social-media page?

Not in V1. Platform pages may require official APIs, exports, authentication, or may restrict downloading. Downio does not bypass those controls.

Does Downio store my videos?

V1 streams direct media through the request path and does not intentionally maintain a video library. Production deployments should add explicit retention and privacy controls.

How will Downio make money?

The planned model is free utility traffic first, followed by compliant advertising and optional premium features once usage justifies the operating cost.
